Simple, tiered verification

Start with the basics. Provide more identification as you move up to larger amounts.

Tier 1

  • Phone number
  • BVN
  • National ID card
  • House address
  • Deposit of $200 to $400

Higher tiers

Larger amounts and larger loans need more identification. For a bigger loan, a referral is also needed after a successful investment. You receive a reward of $50 to $100, depending on the number of referrals.

Rules that protect every member

Breach of contract

10% or 30% of your accumulated capital is deducted, depending on the amount you have built up.

Loan default

Your cards are frozen and your investments are restricted.

Questions

Why can’t I withdraw early?

The long-term plan is a strict savings plan. You choose how many years your money stays invested, and it is locked until then. If a serious situation comes up, speak to headquarters, who can approve early access.

How is my loan limit decided?

The limit depends on your savings. After 6 months of contributions you can borrow 2 to 3 times what you have saved. This keeps the pool protected against default.

How does the pool grow?

Members contribute regularly, and loans to members earn interest. That interest stays in the pool and raises its value for everyone.

How do referral rewards work?

After a successful investment you can refer someone to qualify for a higher loan, and you receive a reward of $50 to $100, depending on the number of referrals.
